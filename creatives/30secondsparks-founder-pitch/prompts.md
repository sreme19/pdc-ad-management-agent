# Prompts — 30secondsparks-founder-pitch

Per `rules/creative-generation.md` §2 the plate carries no words; all type is set in `build.py`.

## Opening claims — three separate generations (Grok Imagine)

One man per generation, not one clip containing three. A single generation of three different men
drifts or repeats the same face, and a failure then costs all three rather than one. They must read
as different from each other **and** from the founder, who appears on camera at 10.6s.

Hard constraints from `rules/compliance.md` §6.2 (the app owner's own 21 Aug note): **Indian
models**, no visible AI-tool watermark, no AI glitches. §7: Grok stamps its wordmark low-right —
crop **90px** off the bottom, not Flow's 150.

Permitted because §6.2's settled scope allows a generated person to appear photorealistic,
unlabelled, addressing camera. The prohibited form is a generated person narrating a first-person
experience of using Riteangle — not the case here: the narration is the founder's own voice and the
type states claims men make generally, not testimony about the product.

**man-a** — `He says he's a founder.`
> Photorealistic vertical video portrait, Indian man in his early 30s, clean-shaven, smart casual
> navy shirt, standing in a modern Bengaluru co-working space, warm daylight, looking directly into
> camera with a confident easy smile, subtle natural motion, shallow depth of field, no text, no
> logos, no watermark

**man-b** — `Six foot.`
> Photorealistic vertical video portrait, tall Indian man in his late 20s, light stubble, white
> linen shirt, standing on an urban rooftop at golden hour, looking directly into camera, relaxed
> confident posture, subtle natural motion, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos, no watermark

**man-c** — `Well travelled.`
> Photorealistic vertical video portrait, Indian man in his mid 30s, short beard, olive jacket and
> backpack, standing in a sunlit airport terminal, looking directly into camera with a slight
> smile, subtle natural motion, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos, no watermark

Save as `_source/grok-man-{a,b,c}.mp4`. About 2s of usable motion each is enough; the build trims.

## Alternative to the portraits, if generation disappoints

A dating profile card on the cream ground — a name, a placeholder avatar, and the claims arriving as
profile fields — with "Nobody checks." landing on the card itself. Arguably stronger than three
faces: it shows the object the lie actually lives in, costs no credits, carries no compliance
surface, and rhymes with the product screens five seconds later. Not yet built.

## Music

Flow Music, generated after the picture is locked, per the app owner's decision on 7 Sep. §7b: a
single bed across the whole cut, never one short clip looped, and **the finished mix is never
loudness-normalised** — each bed to its own target, then peak-limit only.

The archived beds in `../girlboss-moodboard-w1830/_source/videos/assembly/audio/` are NOT reused:
`shotlist.md:55` flags consumer Flow Music output as an ambiguous commercial licence. This is an
organic post rather than a paid placement, which is a different exposure, but a fresh generation
costs credits the founder has and removes the question.
