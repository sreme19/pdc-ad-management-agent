## Read on the concept

The through-line Sree is gathering: **aspirational-woman aesthetic** (boss energy + quiet
luxury) crossed with the **relatable meme-caption voice** the competitor D2C brands
(newme, savana) use to sell to the same young-woman audience. The open question is how
that aesthetic ties to the app's actual value prop for women — confirm the angle before
it hardens into a brief.

## Concept / emotional spine (Sree's audio notes — IN PROGRESS, still being dictated)

The bridge from the aesthetic to the app is **emotional**, not aspirational-flex:

- The pain: women in relationships absorb being **humiliated, ignored, left alone, not
  valued, not given attention** — the emotions that "kill the soul from inside."
- The turn: *you're not here to keep taking these negative emotions in — you deserve a
  bigger life.*
- The "bigger life" is what the mood-board imagery depicts (boss energy, luxury, freedom,
  girlfriends, self-worth). The board is the *destination*, the payoff — not the hook.
- Form: a **montage** that walks a woman from the low → the rise.

### Creative principles (Sree's notes)

1. **No sexual framing — this is a targeting decision, not just taste.** Sexualized
   imagery pulls *men* into the lead form, which is exactly the wrong lead. Keep it out.
   - **First ~10–12 seconds:** no sexual elements at all. Avoid chest/breast framing.
     Face is okay; don't sexualize it. This is the primary rule for the opening.
   - **Later in the video:** a full-body woman "looking hot / powerful" can come in as
     part of the "bigger life" payoff — aspirational, not sexual.
   - ⚠️ Reconcile the later "looking hot" beat against principle #1 and
     `rules/compliance.md` when we brief — keep it aspiration/power, not sex appeal, so
     it doesn't re-open the men-attracting problem or trip Snap policy.

### The creative engine — the "reframe" beat (core device)

The ad should create a **female-desire trigger** — the way a hot-woman image short-circuits
a man's decision to sign up, but built from **aspirational identity** (the "bigger life"
self), not sex. A woman sees who she could be and wants in.

Delivered as a **montage of reframe beats**. Each beat:
- **"People think this is who I am"** → the negative label she gets in relationships
  (overly sensitive, too emotional, bossy, demanding… — pull from the pain-point research).
- **"…but this is what I actually am / this is where my real value is"** → the
  aspirational truth, shown by a mood-board visual (the bigger life).

Example Sree gave: over the private-jet "Our Future: Billionaire" shot (`board-07`/`08`) —
*"People think I'm overly sensitive and emotional… no, I'm building myself. I'm going to
be a billionaire one day. This is where my value is."*

Copy is mostly **English**. Structure repeats: label → reframe → aspirational payoff image.

### Beats being built (Sree picks a pain-point → we flip it; Hinglish)

Each beat = label → flipped reframe, over an aspirational visual. Copy is Hinglish.

**TONE (global): sarcastic bend on every beat.** Take the label/insult and hand it back
with a smirk. Not earnest/inspirational — clap-back energy.

**Beat 1 — "High-maintenance" → high-VALUE (self-investment)**
- Pain-point #5 (too demanding / high-maintenance).
- Visual: girl shopping / splurging on herself — reads as *choosing herself*, not being
  spoiled by a man (keeps it empowerment, not provider-energy → compliance-safe).
- Reframe drafts:
  - "Log kehte hain main high-maintenance hoon…" → "Nahi. Main high-value hoon. Main khud
    pe invest karti hoon."
  - "You think I'm too demanding?" → "I'm not demanding. I just know what I'm worth — and
    I stopped settling for less."
- Status: draft, Sree still picking more beats.

**Beat 2 — ₹370 vasooli → "I'm not for sale / out of your league"**
- Pain-point #2 (the "I paid, I'm owed" / ₹370 biryani discourse).
- Visual: girl with Louis Vuitton bag in a luxury car (mood board has the G-wagon + LV shot).
- Reframe drafts (sarcastic):
  - "₹370 ki biryani khilaake socha main so jaungi?" → "Aww. Meri LV bag uska 10x hai.
    Utni toh teri monthly salary bhi nahi."
  - "You spent ₹370 and expected me in return?" → "Cute. This bag costs ten times that.
    Is that even your salary for the month?"
- Compliance guard: keep the flex on HER self-earned value (she bought the bag) = not for
  sale — NOT "I want a rich man" (that re-opens provider-energy / men-attracting + trips
  `rules/compliance.md`).

**Beat 3 — "You're not like other girls" → worn as a badge**
- Pain-point: the "not like other girls" leash (pits women against each other to keep her
  compliant). He means "behave like the ones who adjust"; she flips it to a compliment.
- Visual: she stands apart from a group / the "Corporate Baddie" lineup where one woman
  owns the frame — literally not like the others.
- Reframe drafts (sarcastic):
  - "'Tum other girls jaisi nahi ho.'" → "Thank you. Baaki adjust karti hain — main apni
    value jaanti hoon."
  - "'You're not like other girls.'" → "Correct. Most settle. I don't. That's not an
    insult — that's the point."
