# Script (as entered in Flow Storyboard Studio) — "Tumse Na Ho Paayega"

Final scene-labelled screenplay used to build the storyboard in Google Flow (Storyboard
Studio, Style = Realistic). Scenes 1–7 tagged with the "Scene" format; each heading became a
storyboard scene. Cast: PRIYA (single character carried across all scenes) + WOMEN FRIENDS
(scene 6 only). NO dialog — all Hinglish is overlay type added later. NO on-screen text baked
into frames.

```
TUMSE NA HO PAAYEGA

SCENE 1 — INT. PRIYA'S BEDROOM, SMALL INDIAN TOWN - MORNING
Priya, 23, a natural Indian girl-next-door with warm brown skin and shoulder-length dark hair, in a plain cotton kurti, sits at a simple dressing table clipping an earring, calm and unbothered. Soft warm morning daylight through cotton curtains; cream walls.

SCENE 2 — INT. PRIYA'S BEDROOM - CONTINUOUS
Tighter on Priya. She turns to the camera as a small, knowing half-smile arrives on her face. Same warm morning light.

MATCH CUT TO:  [optional, 3→4 — the small-town→city ascent as one continuous move]

SCENE 3 — EXT. SMALL-TOWN LANE - DAY
Priya, in a plain kurti and jeans with a cloth tote on her shoulder, steps out of a narrow small-town lane into open light, head high; a modest city skyline is hinted far behind. Warm late-morning daylight.

SCENE 4 — EXT. CITY STREET - DAY
Priya, in smart-casual trousers and a simple top, walks a contemporary Indian city footpath with calm purpose, chin level. Ordinary shops and trees, no luxury storefronts. Bright warm daylight.

SCENE 5 — INT. CAFE - DAY
Priya rises from a cafe table and turns to walk out, unbothered and self-possessed. A bright, ordinary Indian cafe; a simple table with a half-finished plate. Warm daylight through the windows.

DISSOLVE TO:  [optional, 5→6 — the glow-up / grade-warm transformation]

SCENE 6 — EXT. ROOFTOP TERRACE - GOLDEN AFTERNOON
Priya, now glowed-up with done makeup, styled hair and a stylish well-cut outfit, radiant and confident, shares a genuine laugh with two or three women friends. Potted plants; warm city behind. Golden daylight.

SCENE 7 — EXT. SUNLIT PROMENADE - DAY
Priya, in her most striking put-together look with full makeup and styled hair, takes the first confident stride forward toward bright daylight and the horizon. The brightest, warmest grade; main-character energy.
```

## Character asset (Flow Assets tab)
- **Priya — Visual Description:** 23-yr-old Indian woman from a small town, natural girl-next
  -door (not a glossy model), warm brown skin, expressive dark eyes, shoulder-length dark hair,
  warm confident half-smile. Plain, contemporary, unbranded clothing. Unambiguously adult.
  Realistic, photographic; warm daylight; cream/warm tones.
- **Priya — Clothing & Accessories (base/anchor look):** plain well-fitting cotton kurti in a
  warm neutral/cream tone, minimal everyday jewellery (small gold studs, one thin bangle),
  natural understated makeup, flat sandals; nothing branded, no logos, no luxury.
- WATCH: her wardrobe should EVOLVE (glowed-up in sc 6–7 per scene text). If the tool locks
  this base outfit across every frame, fix sc 6–7 at storyboard/Grok stage.
