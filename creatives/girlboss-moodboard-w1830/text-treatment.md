# Text treatment — "You Can't Afford Me" (working title)

**Concept:** girlboss / luxury-lifestyle mood board → Snap lead video for `riteangle`,
MOVE-ON lead funnel, women 18–30. Draft, local only. Not proposed, not pushed.
Built with Sree in a live session 2026-08-30. See `_source/README.md` for the mood board,
the emotional spine, tone, and beat derivation; `painpoints-research.md` for the pain-point
research the beats are picked from.

## The device
Each beat = a character label women hear in relationships → flipped **sarcastically** into
a statement of her value, over an aspirational visual. "People think this is who I am… but
this is what I actually am, and this is where my value is."

## Rules honored (from creative-generation.md / compliance.md / house practice)
- 9:16, ~22–24s. 100% **on-screen type** — nobody narrates a first-person Riteangle
  experience (compliance). Silent but for a music bed (Meta Sound Collection / Flow audio).
- **First ~12s: zero sexual framing** (Sree's targeting rule — sexual imagery pulls MEN
  into the form; the 98/2 failure). Face ok, no chest/breast framing. Glam / full-body
  "powerful" shots only in the back half.
- Romanised Hinglish (audience signal). No AI-tool watermark. Type in safe zones.
- POV stays female throughout; CTA = the next step of her bigger life, NOT "find a man" and
  NOT provider-energy / money-as-attraction.

## ⚠️ COMPLIANCE PIVOT (2026-08-30) — ACCEPTED BY SREE — money/luxury version does NOT ship

The first-drafted engine ("you can't afford me", "out of budget", "bag costs 10x your
salary", jets/G-wagon/LV visuals) trips **compliance.md Rule #1 — money is never an
attraction signal**, which bans money/wealth/luxury as a desirability signal in copy AND
"visually (no luxury cars, no cash…)". `creative-style.md`: a line that trips compliance
doesn't ship. So the concept was re-based:
- **Currency swapped:** rupees/luxury → standards, self-respect, freedom, ambition.
- **Visuals swapped:** dark luxury flex → warm-daylight *thriving* (also lands on cream,
  the brand's deliberate in-feed differentiator; §5 creative-generation forbids the
  dark-nightclub default).
- **Refrain swapped:** "You can't afford me" (money) → "Tumse na ho paayega" / "You can't
  keep up" (capability, not cash). PENDING Sree's pick.
The sarcastic label→flip engine and the low→rise arc are unchanged — only the currency is.

### Accepted angle (Sree, 2026-08-30) — self-authored growth, man as instrument
The "bigger life" = **growth, experiences, adventure** the woman pursues for herself. She's
capable and can attract anyone, so the value flows FROM her — whoever she meets on the app is
an **instrument to her journey, not the source of it.** App = where she meets someone who fits
the life she's already building, on her terms; NOT someone who funds/spoils it.
- Thesis line: **"Jo milega, mere safar ka hissa banega — meri manzil nahi."**
- Keeps POV female, no couple/provider shot, no money — compliant AND on riteangle's real
  "build yourself" positioning.

### EXPLICITLY EXCLUDED (do not re-introduce)
"Join → attract generous men → get spoiled → luxury life," sugar-daddy/kept-woman framing,
"as luxury as possible" visuals, luxury cars/bags/jets/cash. Raised and set aside 2026-08-30:
trips compliance Rule #1 (bans *implying/visually signalling* money, generosity, being kept),
re-opens the 98/2 men-flood, and flips empowerment → gold-digger. The provider-energy
preference is served by the MATCHING BACKEND only, never the creative.

## Sequence (compliant, slower pacing — readability was the failure last time)

Pacing rule this cut: **each text beat holds ~3.5–4s**, one idea per screen, no rapid
stacking. Last video read too fast to keep up (Sree). 4 flips, not 5, to avoid overstuffing.

FINAL COMPRESSED CUT — 8 screens, ~24s (under Sree's 25s target), ~3s dwell each for
readability. 4 flips. Refrain "Tumse na ho paayega." Worth RECOGNIZED, never PROVIDED.

### The girl (one cast, carried across all scenes via frame-into-Grok pipeline)
~22–25, relatable Tier-2/3 Indian — the girl-next-door who levels up, NOT a glam model

## Why the text works
- Two-part reveal each beat: insult appears (his voice) → struck/replaced by her flip (her
  voice). Same rhythm ×4 — viewer learns the game by Beat 2, anticipates the clapback by 4.
- Sarcasm sits in the SECOND line every time ("Cute.", "Thank you.", "You can't afford me.").
- Low→rise compressed: labels = the low (soul-drain), flips = the rise, TURN = the thesis.
