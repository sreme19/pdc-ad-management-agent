## CLIP 1 — the drain begins (0:00–0:05)

Type overlay (Thai): **`เท.`** → **`โปรไฟล์ปลอม`**

```
A quiet documentary close shot of @Aom alone on her bed late at night in an
ordinary Thai apartment, lit almost entirely by the cool blue-white light of the
phone in her hands. She is scrolling slowly with her thumb, not reading anything
properly, the specific flat expression of someone who has been doing this for
forty minutes and is not enjoying it. She lets out a breath. She is completely
absorbed and unaware of the camera.

Light: cool, low, blue-white phone glow as the key light on her face and hands.
The room falls away into warm darkness behind her. Desaturated, flat contrast.

Camera: 50mm, shallow depth of field, EYE LEVEL from slightly to one side, so we
are beside her and not looking at her. Head and hands in frame. Very slow push
in. Natural skin texture, visible pores, no retouching.

IMPORTANT: the phone screen must be pure flat white and completely blank — no
interface, no text, no icons, no status bar. It is a placeholder to be
composited afterwards.

Composition: she sits in the lower two thirds with dark negative space above for
type. Keep the top 14% and bottom 20% free of important detail — Reels UI covers
them. Vertical 9:16.

EXCLUDE: any text, watermark, logo or interface; any man anywhere including the
background; a second person; anyone who could read as a teenager; crying;
melodrama; a posed sad expression held for the camera; looking into the lens;
cropped tops or bare midriffs; luxury or designer signifiers; black and white.
```

Same "not sad" caution as the source: name the emotion positively — flat, unenjoying, forty minutes in,
bored and faintly disgusted, never tearful.

**Every line above is a machine draft. No native Thai speaker in the target register (women 18–30,
casual/dating-app-literate) has read this copy.** The source pack's Hinglish carried the identical
warning and it still hasn't been closed as of this cut — don't let this one go the same way. This is a
**spend gate**: generate and typeset against these lines, but hold the ad-set budget until a native
speaker signs off on register, not just literal correctness (e.g. whether `เท` reads as casual-current
slang to an 18–30 audience or as dated/wrong).

**Fonts:** Gabarito is Latin-only and cannot set Thai script. Pair it with a Thai-supporting face for
the type layer — **Noto Sans Thai** or **IBM Plex Sans Thai** are the closest free matches to
Gabarito's geometric-grotesque weight — and keep `riteangle` itself in Gabarito/Latin regardless, since
the brand mark's lowercase pun (`creative-style.md`) doesn't survive translation or a different
typeface. Thai script has its own line-height and diacritic-stacking behaviour (tone marks and vowels
sit above/below the consonant); test the type layer at actual Story size before treating a card as
final — the same "legible at phone size, not desktop zoom" gate the source pack applies (§10.6).
