## The three rules this pack is built on

**1. No dialogue, and that is what makes the format shippable.** `compliance.md` §6.2 bars a
generated woman from narrating a first-person experience of Riteangle — the exact thing that blocked
the MOVE-ON video while clearing the still. Silence removes the first person entirely. Every word
lives in overlaid type, which is brand voice. **If anyone adds a spoken line later, this pack is
blocked again.**

**2. No man in frame, anywhere, in any clip.** Including the background, including out of focus.

**3. She is never the object of the frame.** This is the hard one and it is where a "girls going
through breakups" brief drifts. A beautiful woman arranged to look sad *at us* is a lookbook, and
`creative-generation.md` §1 traces the 98/2 male-submission split to exactly that. Round-05 slot 3
had to be rewritten for it.

**The mechanism that keeps it safe: show the drain from behind her eyes, not from in front of her
face.** The dead chat, the typing dots that stop, her own hand, her own screen. Where a face appears
she is absorbed in her own moment and unaware of the camera — never presenting, never to lens.

**Corollary — the drains are named in TYPE, not acted out.** The clip does not have to depict
catfishing or cheating; the word does that. This is what keeps "cheating" shootable at all, since
depicting it would need a man in frame.

## The colour argument — deliberate deviation from round-05

Round-05's plates are all high-key warm cream, "never grey and never dark." That is that pack's
direction for a landing-page hero, not a rule in `rules/`.

**This cut inverts it on purpose: the drains are cool, flat and dim — phone-light on her face, late,
alone — and the flip goes warm golden daylight.** The energy metaphor becomes literally visible, and
the turn lands as a change in the light rather than only as a change in the type. Losing that
contrast would make four clips of the same warm cream carry an argument about depletion, which they
cannot.

# STAGE A — cast first (do this before any clip)

Left nav → **Characters** → create from description → generate → check the face reads
**clearly mid-twenties** → save under the name given. Recall with `@name` in every clip prompt.

**Tone direction, 2026-08-29: UPBEAT.** The app owner's note — the register is wry and quick, not
mournful. That changes casting: every face here is cast for *animation and humour*, someone whose
default is amused. A woman cast for sadness plays the drain section as a lament; a woman cast for
wit plays it as "ugh, been there", which is both more shareable and much less likely to read as an
object of the frame. Cast the joke, not the grief.

**Ages are written into every prompt and are deliberately mid-twenties.** The audience runs 18–30;
the depiction stays unambiguously adult (`compliance.md` §6.3). If a face reads under 25, regenerate
— do not ship it and hope.

## Casting notes that save a round

- **"From a big Indian city", not a named one.** Round-05 named Bangalore in every prompt because
  Flow's backgrounds default Western — that fix is still needed, but it belongs in the CLIP prompts
  where the setting is. Naming a city in a character description bakes a regional read into a face
  for no benefit, and `q-2026-08-29-the-move-on-funnel-now-aims` is still open on whether this
  campaign is metro or not. Keep the faces geography-neutral and decide the setting per clip.
- **Do not upload a photo and do not build an avatar from one** (`compliance.md` §6.1).
- **Regenerate on age before anything else.** It is the one gate with no argument attached.
- **Check hands at casting time.** A character saved with a bad hand carries it into every clip.

## CLIP 1 — the drain begins (0:00–0:05)

Type overlay: **`Ghosting.`** → **`Fake profiles.`**

```
A quiet documentary close shot of @Meher alone on her bed late at night in an
ordinary Indian apartment, lit almost entirely by the cool blue-white light of
the phone in her hands. She is scrolling slowly with her thumb, not reading
anything properly, the specific flat expression of someone who has been doing
this for forty minutes and is not enjoying it. She lets out a breath. She is
completely absorbed and unaware of the camera.

Light: cool, low, blue-white phone glow as the key light on her face and hands.
The room falls away into warm darkness behind her. Desaturated, flat contrast.

Camera: 50mm, shallow depth of field, EYE LEVEL from slightly to one side, so we
are beside her and not looking at her. Head and hands in frame. Very slow push
in. Natural skin texture, visible pores, no retouching.

IMPORTANT: the phone screen must be pure flat white and completely blank — no
interface, no text, no icons, no status bar. It is a placeholder to be
composited afterwards.

Composition: she sits in the lower two thirds with dark negative space above for
type. Keep the top 14% and bottom 20% free of important detail — Reels UI covers
them. Vertical 9:16.

EXCLUDE: any text, watermark, logo or interface; any man anywhere including the
background; a second person; anyone who could read as a teenager; crying;
melodrama; a posed sad expression held for the camera; looking into the lens;
cropped tops or bare midriffs; luxury or designer signifiers; black and white.
```

**Note on "not sad," and the upbeat direction.** You cannot prompt an absence — round 04 rendered
"not sad" as sad. The positive instruction is *flat, unenjoying, forty minutes in* — **bored and
faintly disgusted, never tearful.** Per the app owner's 2026-08-29 note the whole cut runs upbeat, so
the target for clips 1 and 2 is the eye-roll, not the lament: she is unimpressed, not wounded. If a
generation comes back tearful or mournful, re-roll toward **exasperated**. A sad-girl montage is also
the version most likely to fail the object-of-the-frame gate, so this is a craft note and a
compliance note at once.

## CLIP 4 — the flip (0:14–0:20)

Type overlay: **`Date mat karo. Bas milo, aur jiyo.`** → **`Apply karo →`**

The payoff. Full warmth, full motion, three of them, and nobody is dating anybody.

```
A candid documentary photograph in motion of @Meher, @Ira and @Nandita walking
three abreast along a tree-lined residential footpath in an Indian city in the
last hour of afternoon sun, mid-stride and in step, mid-conversation. @Ira has
turned her head to say something to @Meher, @Meher is laughing at it, @Nandita is
looking ahead and grinning at what she is hearing. They are talking to each
other, not to the camera. All three are adults in their twenties.

Light: low warm golden sun from behind the camera, throwing three long soft
shadows forward across the pavement. Bright, warm, high-key — the complete
opposite of clips 1 and 2.

Setting: unmistakably an Indian residential street — plastered compound walls, a
painted gate, dappled leaf shadow, a parked scooter far behind them.

Wardrobe: contemporary Indian urban streetwear, unbranded, full-length tops —
coral, warm pink and burnt orange. Canvas totes.

Camera: 35mm, EYE LEVEL, straight on, from about four metres ahead, tracking
backwards with them. Full figures. Slight motion blur in hands and hair.
Photojournalistic, natural skin texture, no retouching.

Composition: the three of them fill the middle of the frame walking toward the
viewer, shadows leading in from the bottom. Keep the top 14% and bottom 20% clear.
Vertical 9:16.

EXCLUDE: any text, watermark, logo or legible signage; any man; a fourth person;
anyone who could read as a teenager; posing in a row for a group photo; anyone
looking into the lens; cropped tops or bare midriffs; a bar or nightclub;
alcohol; luxury or designer signifiers — no expensive cars, no resort, no
cruise, no shopping bags; black and white; low camera angles.
```

**The EXCLUDE list on this clip is doing compliance work, not taste work.** `compliance.md` #1
forbids money, luxury and provider framing as an attraction signal, and the iOS build was already
rejected under Apple Guideline 1.1.4 for reading as compensated dating. "Living your life" is the
compliant version of the app owner's original framing **only** while the experience is plainly hers
and plainly unbought. A resort or a cruise deck in this frame reinstates the rejected claim without
a word of copy.

Alternate opener if clip 1 needs a stronger first frame: **`Swipe karte karte saal nikal gaya.`**

**Fonts:** Gabarito carries the lowercase `riteangle` mark and is Latin-only. Romanised Hindi sets in
Gabarito fine. If any Devanagari is used, pair with Mukta or Noto Sans Devanagari — but
`lrn-2026-08-29-roman-script-is-an-audience-signal` says Devanagari signals a Hindi-heartland or
older audience, so romanised is the default and Devanagari is a separate test.

**Every line is unvalidated.** No native speaker in the target register has read this copy. That is a
spend gate, not a production gate — generate the clips, hold the buy.

## Music

No dialogue means the track carries the pacing. A commercial track is a licensing problem on a paid
ad — use Meta's own Sound Collection or Flow-generated audio. Most Reels are watched muted, so the
cut must work silent: the type has to carry the whole argument on its own.
