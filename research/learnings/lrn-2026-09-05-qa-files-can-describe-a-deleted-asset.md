## Claim

A creative folder's qa.md can describe a file that is no longer in the folder, and nothing detects it. creatives/moveon-lead-w1830/qa.md QA'd 'energy-kahan-jaati-hai-ROUGH-v3.mp4' on 2026-08-29 and recorded a smeared-over watermark plus three craft defects (whited-out eyes ~1.8s, duplicated office frame with a visible seam 11-13s, warped fingers ~14s). The file actually in the folder, asset-a.mp4, is a 2026-09-01 re-cut in which ALL FOUR are absent. The QA text names no filename in its gate lines, no hash and no date per asset, so a reader has no way to tell the evidence is stale - and it reads as a blocking record against an asset that is in fact clean.

## Evidence

- (2026-09-05) Verified 2026-09-05 frame by frame at the exact timestamps qa.md names: full 1fps watermark scan across all 20s (16 of 20 frames scored a flat 0; the four above it resolve to a lit phone screen at 4s and a sunlit arm against a black chair 9-13s), plus extracted frames at 1.8s (eyes closed, normally rendered), 11.5s (single clean office frame, no seam) and 14.2s (hands clean). Recorded as a dated comparison table in that qa.md rather than by editing the original reviewer's verdict.
