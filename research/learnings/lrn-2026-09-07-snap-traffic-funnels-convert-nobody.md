## Claim

Every Snap TRAFFIC funnel into /get/w has produced zero signups, across 1,287 tracked page views and 43 store clicks from three separate squads. The one Snap funnel that converts is the LEAD funnel, which produced 13 signups from 36 store clicks - comparable store-click volume, categorically different outcome.

## Evidence

- (2026-09-07) Reconciled 2026-09-07 across every live Snap record, joining Snap ad-level lifetime stats to marketing_page_views and user_acquisition. TRAFFIC arm, all into /get/w: squad 5d617ab4 1,095 page views / 37 store clicks, squad 1b993192 100 / 2, squad dee446d1 92 / 4. Total 1,287 page views, 43 store clicks, Rs 938.62 spend, ZERO signups. LEAD arm, squad 85c2e782 into /get/w-apply: 45 page views, 36 store clicks, 13 signups, Rs 1,472.26 - Rs 113.25 per signup, against budget.md's ~Rs 200/signup target for women, so UNDER target. CONFIDENCE IS ASYMMETRIC AND THAT IS DELIBERATE: the zero is high-confidence on n=1,287, far above MIN_SAMPLE. The 13 is directional only, below the 30 gate, and no single creative can claim it (see lrn-2026-09-07-shared-squad-destroyed-per-ad-attribution). WHAT IT SUGGESTS BUT DOES NOT PROVE: the instant form is doing real work - pre-qualifying, capturing contact before the store, and giving a reason to continue - that a page with a store button does not. The 2026-09-05 direct-to-store traffic ad removed even the page, and at 1,023 swipes it too reports zero installs and zero signups. Three funnel shapes, one converts.
